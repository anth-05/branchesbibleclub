Closer to Christ, together at the table.

Branches Bible Club is a women’s Bible study: a circle of women who open the Bible together every other Thursday. We read, we talk (a lot), we pray, and over coffee and candles we pursue one thing: a stronger intimacy with Christ.

Why we’re called Branches

“I am the vine. You are the branches.”

Christ is the true Vine and we are the branches. Whoever remains in Him bears much fruit. That's why every member of BBC is called a Branch: we only grow when we stay close to Him.

John 15:5 · World English Bible

What we learned

After each Bible study we post the main lessons here, so you can look back, or catch up if you missed a Thursday.

01Session 1

Song of Songs 1:1–4

The pursuit of intimacy

  1. iIntimacy requires intentional pursuit. Closeness doesn’t happen by accident: the bride seeks, asks and runs after Him.
  2. iiLove wants to know more. Real love carries a desire to know someone more deeply.
  3. iiiBeyond routine. A relationship with God goes beyond religious habits into real presence.
To reflect on & pray

Am I pursuing God’s presence, or simply His blessings?

What does intentional time with Christ look like in my life?

What needs to change in my relationship with Him?

PrayerLord, give me a deeper hunger for Your presence.

Questions you might have

When do you meet?

Every other Thursday. We’ll email you the exact time and place when you sign up.

What should I bring?

Your Bible, a notebook and yourself. There’s a cup of coffee with your name on it.

What happens at a gathering?

We read a Bible passage together, share what it stirs in us and pray for one another.

I missed a Thursday. Can I catch up?

Yes. After each gathering we post the main lessons on this page, under “What we learned”.

Which Bible translation do you use?

On this site we quote the Statenvertaling (Dutch) and the World English Bible (English).
